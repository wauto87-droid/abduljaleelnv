My Philosophy

Not just a coder.
I understand Business.

Many developers build software that is "technically" correct but useless in the real world. I come from real business backgrounds, not just theory.

My strength is in breaking down a messy business process into simple steps, removing the waste, and building a system that your normal staff can actually use.


My Core Beliefs

  • Simplicity: Systems should reduce dependency, not increase it.
  • Usability: If your staff can't use it, it's a failure.
  • Value: I don't sell software. I build working systems.
